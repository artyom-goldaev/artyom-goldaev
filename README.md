I am developing an open project dedicated to exploring and developing an alternative model of society based on mutual creation, voluntary participation, and the fair use and distribution of resources.

## Society of Mutual Creation

What if society could function without money?

What if human activity were driven not only by individual interests, but also by the desire to meet mutual needs and create common well-being?

Society of Mutual Creation is an open project exploring these questions and seeking practical answers.

The concept is based on several fundamental principles:

- **A moneyless system** — exchange based on the results of people's work and production.
- **Energy accounting** — electricity as a universal reference for transparent accounting of energy resources, not as money or currency.
- **Fair access to resources and goods** — balancing individual contributions with common needs.
- **Decentralized self-governance** — without centralized control or concentration of power.
- **Mutual creation** — people as participants in creating common well-being, not merely as instruments of profit.
- **Rational resource use** — production and consumption guided by actual needs and respect for the environment.

This is not a finished model offered for acceptance. It is an open initiative that needs discussion, criticism, research, and collective development.
